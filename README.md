# LumaView TV | Business Performance Analysis

## Overview

LumaView TV is a fictional European pay-TV provider operating in Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg.

This project examines the company's financial and subscriber performance from January 2022 to October 2026 to assess its profitability, growth, and long-term business viability in an increasingly competitive entertainment market.

## Business Questions

- Is the company growing or declining in revenue, subscribers, and profitability?
- Which subscription tiers are gaining or losing customers?
- Which packages contribute most to billed and collected revenue?
- Which countries perform best and worst across subscription tiers?
- Are new subscriptions sufficient to offset cancellations?
- What are the main risks to the company's financial sustainability?

## Data

The analysis uses a synthetic dataset of monthly subscription performance across three countries and three subscription tiers.

The dataset includes subscriber movements, pricing, billed and collected revenue, and operating costs.

**Period:** January 2022 – October 2026

## Tech Stack

- **Python (pandas):** Data cleaning, transformation, and analysis
- **Plotly Express:** Interactive data visualisation
- **Streamlit:** Interactive dashboard
- **Git & GitHub:** Version control

## Methodology

1. Data inspection and quality validation
2. Data cleaning and transformation
3. KPI calculations and exploratory analysis
4. Interactive visualisation and dashboard development
5. Business findings and recommendations
